title: Welcome to BurnerBlock
description: Learn how BurnerBlock helps you protect your SaaS.
---

BurnerBlock is a developer-first email verification and signup abuse prevention platform.

It helps SaaS founders prevent:
- Disposable email signups
- Temporary email abuse
- Gmail alias abuse
- Free-trial abuse
- Fake account creation

Developers integrate BurnerBlock into their signup flow using a simple API.
