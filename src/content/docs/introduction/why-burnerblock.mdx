title: Why BurnerBlock
description: Why you should use BurnerBlock for your SaaS.
---

BurnerBlock is built for developers who want to stop abuse without adding friction to real users.

### Key Benefits
- **Higher Lead Quality:** Ensure you're only getting real users.
- **Save Costs:** Don't pay for fake users in your database or email marketing tools.
- **Prevent Free-Trial Abuse:** Stop users from creating multiple accounts using disposable emails.
